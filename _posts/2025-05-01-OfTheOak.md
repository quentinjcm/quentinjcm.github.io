Of The Oak is an immersive installation celebrating the oak tree as a network of relationships. Through an interactive video, multichannel audio, and an online field guide, the artwork invites viewers on a sensory journey across seasons, unveiling the oak’s hidden vibrancy.

<https://marshmallowlaserfeast.com/project/of-the-oak/>

Once again building on systems developed for previous MLF tree portraits, this time with the added wrinkle of a real time particle layer running over the top. The system developed by Chris and Sam runs in unity to use two Z Cameras to generate velocity maps that then drive a particle system that interacts with the tree and ground and is live composited into a pre-rendered video with a depth map.

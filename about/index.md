Quentin Corker-Marin is a London based artist and filmmaker. She studied Computer Visualisation and Animation at Bournemouth University, graduating in 2017. Since then, she has balanced commercial projects with her own artistic practice, working as an in-house artist at Glassworks VFX and then Marshmallow Laser Feast before beginning her freelance career in 2022. She enjoys working across commercials, TV, film and fine art, blending digital and tactile mediums, and refusing to distinguish between technical and artistic problem solving.

Quentin is currently available on a freelance basis. If you would like to get in touch for any reason, please email her at <quentin.corkermarin@gmail.com>. You can also find her CV [here](/cv/).
